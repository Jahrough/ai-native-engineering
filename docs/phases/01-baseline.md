# Phase 1 — Baseline assessment and engineering audit

## Purpose

Establish an honest starting point, identify development priorities, and choose a safe, realistic environment for practice. This phase prevents the curriculum from assuming skills or experience that have not been assessed.

## Outcomes

- A dated baseline across engineering, AI use, architecture, security, operations, and learning habits.
- A concise audit of strengths, gaps, constraints, and goals.
- A prioritized and feasible learning plan with a practice environment and verification approach.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish demonstrated capability from familiarity or confidence.
- Assess current workflows, software delivery practices, AI usage, and risk awareness.
- Choose measurable learning goals and revisit them using evidence.

## Prerequisites

None. Answer from actual experience; label unknowns rather than guessing.

## Core competencies

Reflective practice, evidence collection, software delivery awareness, risk identification, goal setting, and learning-plan design.

## Concepts and terminology

Baseline, competency, evidence, confidence calibration, gap analysis, learning objective, audit scope, and reassessment. Confidence is a hypothesis; observed evidence informs the rating.

## Learning progression

- **Foundational:** inventory current practices and identify unknowns.
- **Intermediate:** compare self-ratings with artifacts, outcomes, and reviewer feedback.
- **Advanced:** establish repeatable reassessment and prioritize development against role and system risks.

## Practical exercises

1. Score yourself against the [competency matrix](../competency-matrix.md), marking unknowns separately.
2. Inspect a permitted recent software task: trace requirements, implementation, tests, review, deployment, and operational feedback. Record gaps, not confidential details.
3. Audit current AI use: tasks, data shared, verification, failure cases, permissions, and time/quality effects.
4. Write three specific learning goals with observable evidence and a reassessment date.

## Implementation project

Create a one-page personal engineering audit and a prioritized practice plan. It may be kept privately; do not commit personal or sensitive information here. If no suitable project is accessible, identify the constraints and select a small safe exercise environment.

## Deliverables

- Baseline competency ratings with evidence or an explicit evidence gap.
- Engineering/AI workflow audit and risk notes.
- A sequenced plan and selected practice environment.

## Evaluation criteria

- Ratings distinguish evidence from familiarity and confidence.
- At least three learning goals are observable, feasible, and prioritized.
- AI/data handling risks and practice-environment constraints are documented.
- Another person can understand why the chosen priorities follow from the audit.

## Dependencies and next phase

No dependencies. Phase 1 is the entry point; its baseline informs Phase 2 and later reassessment.

## Recommended resources

- [SFIA skills framework](https://sfia-online.org/en) — reference vocabulary for skills, not a substitute for evidence.
- [Google SRE Book: Embracing Risk](https://sre.google/sre-book/embracing-risk/) — balancing reliability and change.
